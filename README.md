## 📖 About the project

The apex-sensitivity-aim-lab provides a clear way to manage your mouse sensitivity for first-person shooter games. Many players struggle with inconsistent aim when they switch between different games. This tool solves that problem by calculating your exact sensitivity needs. You can analyze your current settings, receive personalized recommendations, and convert your sensitivity values between various titles. The tool runs directly in your browser, which keeps your system clean and makes the setup process fast.

## ⚙️ Features

This application offers tools to help you gain control over your mouse movement.

*   Sensitivity Conversion: Move your preferred sensitivity settings from one game to another with high precision.
*   Aim Practice Integration: Connect your analysis data directly to popular aim trainers to see immediate results.
*   Personalized Recommendations: Receive suggestions for your sensitivity based on your preferred mouse DPI and playstyle.
*   Settings Review: Store and compare your mouse settings across different shooter titles in a single dashboard.
*   Browser-based Utility: Access your data from any machine without the need for complex installations or background processes.

## 🛠️ Usage Instructions

Once you open the tool, you will see a main dashboard. Enter your mouse DPI and your current in-game sensitivity value. The application will process this data and provide a unified sensitivity number. Use this number to update the settings inside your game menu. If you want to switch to a new game, select the target title from the dropdown menu and the tool will calculate the converted value for you. Save your profiles to return to them later without re-entering the numbers.
